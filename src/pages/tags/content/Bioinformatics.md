---
title: 'Bioinformatics'
description: 'Using computation to study biological sequences, protein interactions, structure, and function.'
---

Bioinformatics combines biological questions with computational analysis. I use it to study protein sequences, short linear motifs, evolutionary conservation, structural context, and interaction networks.

My thesis work focused on short linear motifs in amyloidogenic and immune-response proteins. It combined sequence searches, homolog alignments, disorder and structure analysis, statistical null models, and database curation. This work contributed to the **DOC_TBK1_STING_1** class in the 2024 update of the Eukaryotic Linear Motif resource.
